The Lead · Thanksgiving starts at the Masonic Lodge

If you know a family that could use a hand this Thanksgiving, today is the day to pass the word. The Vessel Project is serving a free hot meal and handing out a turkey or ham, plus groceries, to the first 200 families — today, 11 a.m. to 2 p.m., at the Masonic Lodge on V.E. Washington Street in Lake Charles. Just show up. No ticket, no fuss.

It's the kind of thing that makes Thanksgiving week feel like Thanksgiving week around here: neighbors feeding neighbors, a few days before the holiday itself. The volunteers do the cooking and the loading; all anybody has to do is come by. And if your own table is already set, the best thing you can do this morning is tell somebody who needs one.

Thanksgiving is Thursday, and the week is full of chances like this one — free meals, food drives, and folks looking out for each other all the way from Lake Charles to DeRidder. Today's the first of them, and it's a good one.

"Thanksgiving week around here starts the way it should — with somebody setting an extra place at the table."

  • Where: Masonic Lodge, 333 V.E. Washington St., Lake Charles

  • Who: The first 200 families

Featured · A changing of the guard at the police department

Lake Charles Police Chief Shawn Caldwell is retiring at the end of the year. Caldwell announced last Wednesday that December 31 will be his last day, closing out 33 years with the department — the last eight of them as chief, a job he's held since January 2019.

Mayor Marshall Simien says the city will name an interim chief from the department's deputy chief ranks within 30 days, then run a nationwide search for a permanent replacement. Caldwell, who announced the decision in a statement, said he and his wife decided the time was right. Whoever takes the job next inherits a department at a crossroads — and a city that will be watching the search closely. What happens next →

The Guide · Thanksgiving week

Free meals, kids' camps, karaoke, and the first Christmas lights of the season — the whole week, day by day.

Today, Monday:

  • Gobble Games: Swim & Sports Camp, 8 a.m. — a half-day Thanksgiving-break camp for kids ages 6–12 at the SPAR Recreation & Aquatic Center, 933 W. Parish Rd., Sulphur. Swimming, sports, and games.
  • Family Karaoke Mondays, 6 p.m. — take the mic at Rikenjaks Brewing, 3716 Ryan St., Lake Charles.

Tuesday:

  • A Whole Latte Gratitude, 9:30 a.m. — coffee, conversation, and Thanksgiving activities for families at Chennault Coffee Shop, 200 Avenue C, Lake Charles.
  • Iowa community food distribution, 1–3 p.m. — holiday meals for about 300 families in Iowa, Bell City, Lacassine, and LeBleu Settlement, at Lawrence Toups Jr. Memorial Park in Iowa. Pickup only for families already selected — applications closed Friday.
  • Community Thanksgiving Dinner, 5:30–7 p.m. — a free Thanksgiving meal, open to everyone with no registration, at Word of Hope, 3975 E. Prien Lake Rd., Lake Charles.

Wednesday:

  • Thanksgiving crafts & story time, 1–5:30 p.m. — an afternoon at the South Beauregard Branch Library, 7221 La. 12, Ragley: an adult fall-garland craft at 1, children's story time at 2:30, a makers activity at 3:30, and teen DIY fall-tree art at 4:30.
  • Night Before Thanksgiving, 6 p.m. — live music from Analog at The Creole Bar & Grill, DoubleTree by Hilton, 320 Arena Rd., Sulphur.
  • Blackout Wednesday, evening — Rusty Metoyer plays No Boundaries in downtown Lake Charles.

Thursday, Thanksgiving Day:

  • VFW Thanksgiving dinner, 11:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m. — Cole-Miers VFW Post 3619, 1115 Hwy. 27, DeRidder. Thanksgiving dinner for members, auxiliary, active duty, veterans, and their families.

Friday:

  • Black Friday Holiday Market, Friday and Saturday — the Second Annual Black Friday VIP Holiday Market at Prien Lake Mall, with more than 50 vendors and specialty gifts.

Saturday:

  • Light Up the Lake, 1–8 p.m. — Lake Charles kicks off Christmas at the Arcade Amphitheater and lakefront, 1111 Bord du Lac Dr.: a walking parade at 1, live music through the afternoon, the lighting ceremony at 6:15 p.m., a lighted boat parade, and fireworks at 7:30 p.m. Free — bring a blanket or a lawn chair; coolers and pets are not permitted.
  • Sulphur Christmas Market, Saturday — holiday shopping at the West Cal Arena and Event Center in Sulphur.

Business Spotlight · Imperial Calcasieu Museum, Lake Charles

School's out this week, and the Imperial Calcasieu Museum has the kids covered. Its Thanksgiving Art Camp runs Monday through Wednesday, with a morning session (9 a.m. to noon) or an afternoon session (1 to 4 p.m.), for kindergarten through age 12 — $40 a day, all supplies included.

The museum itself is worth a visit any week: more than 60 years serving the five parishes, under the branches of the 375-year-old Sallier Oak at 204 W. Sallier St. Regular hours are Tuesday 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., Wednesday through Friday 10 to 3, and Saturday 10 to 2. The Holiday Train model railroad is running for the season, and the galleries rotate local art and history exhibits all year. Plan a visit →

One Good Thing

Some Lake Charles teenagers spent their Sunday teaching Scouts how to build the future. The SWLA Tech Pirates — the community robotics team, FIRST Team 8808 — brought their competition robot to Camp Edgewood, where Scouts worked toward their robotics merit badge. Team captain Eric Phillips and co-captain Ruth Rohrbeck showed them a machine that holds 68 balls and fires up to 20 a second, then let the Scouts get their hands on it. The team takes students in grades 6 through 12 from all across Southwest Louisiana, and its next competition season starts January 9. Meet the Tech Pirates →
