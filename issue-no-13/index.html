The Lead · Fall finally shows up

If you woke up to fog and a little rain on the window this morning, that's the front knocking. A cold front pushes through Southwest Louisiana today — a few showers this morning, then it clears out — and what's behind it is the first real fall air we've had all month.

Monday tops out around 67 with a low of 47, and the National Weather Service says next week stays dry and cool, with a chilly start to Thanksgiving Day and maybe even a Freeze Watch Thanksgiving morning. After a Saturday that hit 78, that's a proper change of seasons — time to dig the jackets out of the closet and check the porch plants.

So today's the pivot point: warm and a little wet this morning, then the kind of crisp air that makes Thanksgiving week feel like Thanksgiving week. Get the errands done early, and if you've got outdoor plans this evening, the rain should be long gone by then.

Featured · Mistletoe & Moss, last day

Today's the last day of the Junior League's holiday market at the Lake Charles Event Center — general admission 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., then the vendors pack up. If you missed Friday and Saturday, this is the short window: three days of local makers, gifts, and Christmas shopping, all under one roof on the lakefront.

While you're there, the LifeShare blood bus is parked outside the Event Center if you'd like to give blood. Market details →

Business Spotlight · Bayou Rum Distillery, Lacassine

Thanksgiving week is the right time to think about the gift shelf — and the Bayou Rum Distillery in Lacassine makes a good stop. Louisiana Spirits has been distilling rum from Louisiana sugar cane there since 2013, in what's now the largest privately owned rum distillery in the country, and the gift shop is stocked for the holidays.

Tours run Tuesday through Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. — book ahead — and walk you through fermentation to bottling before a tasting at the bar. The tour is $10 and includes a tasting of seven rums. It's closed today and Monday, so put it on the list for the week: 20909 Frontage Rd., just off I-10 in Lacassine. Plan a visit →

One Good Thing

Fifty kids in Lake Charles and Moss Bluff have a real bed to sleep in tonight. Volunteers with Sleep in Heavenly Peace and Mt. Olive Baptist Church delivered 50 beds on Saturday — eight teams loaded up trucks, drove to homes, and set up and made each bed, frame and mattress and bedding and pillow included. The Sulphur chapter keeps a regular build and delivery schedule, and it runs on volunteers and donations. Get involved →
