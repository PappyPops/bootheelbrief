The Lead · Light Up the Lake tonight

There's one night a year when the whole parish seems to end up in the same place, and tonight's the night. Light Up the Lake takes over the lakefront from 1 to 8, and it's the city's official start to Christmas — the Christmas Walking Parade kicks things off at 1, the Lake Charles Community Band plays at 2, Calcashoo at 3, and the Last Minute Big Band at 4:30, all at the Arcade Amphitheater. At 6:15, Mayor Marshall Simien Jr. leads the countdown and the lakefront lights up for the first time this season, then the Lighted Boat Parade glides across the water and fireworks close it out at 7:30, set to music on KBYS 88.3 FM.

The rest of the afternoon is built for wandering. More than 30 local artisans line the Holiday Art Walk, Millennium Park turns into Santa's Workshop with crafts for the kids, the train rides run 4:30 to 8 with cookies, hot cocoa, and snow, and the food trucks stay open all day. Drink sales benefit the Literacy Council of SWLA, so the cocoa does a little good while you're at it. It's all free, parking's free, and the only things to remember are a blanket or a lawn chair — and that coolers and pets stay home.

If you've never been, go once and you'll get it. If you have, you already know why the lakefront fills up before the sun goes down. Get there early, find your spot, and stay for the boats — that's the part people talk about on the way home. The city's full schedule →
