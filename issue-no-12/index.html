The Lead · Battle of the Border, under the lights tonight

Tonight's the night Navarre Stadium has been waiting on. McNeese hosts No. 4 Lamar at 6 p.m. in the Battle of the Border — the season finale, Senior Day, and a rivalry game with real stakes on both sides. The Cowboys will honor 18 seniors before kickoff, then try to spoil the Cardinals' season in front of a home crowd.

Lamar comes in 9-2, riding a four-game win streak and ranked fourth in the country — the highest ranking in program history. A win wraps up the Cardinals' first Southland Conference championship since 1971. But McNeese has a say in that. A Cowboy win means a first conference winning season since 2019, a second straight win over a ranked Lamar team after last year's 21-19 upset in Beaumont, and a whole winter of bragging rights. The Cowboys have won their last two at home, and both of Lamar's losses this year came on the road.

Keep an eye on running back Omiri Wiggins, who leads the Southland in rushing at 808 yards and is chasing the conference rushing title in his final home game. Kickoff is 6 p.m., the game is on ESPN+ and 92.9 The Lake, and tickets start at $14. Get there early for the senior ceremony — and bring a jacket for the walk back to the car. Schedule & tickets →

Battle of the Border at a glance

  • When: Saturday, Nov. 21, 6 p.m. — Senior Day ceremony before kickoff
  • Where: Louis Bonnette Field at Navarre Stadium, 700 E. McNeese St., Lake Charles
  • Who: McNeese (5-6, 4-4 Southland) vs. No. 4 Lamar (9-2, 7-1 Southland) — the 49th meeting; McNeese leads the series 30-17-1
  • Watch/listen: ESPN+ and 92.9 The Lake; tickets from $14
  • Weather at kickoff: Mostly clear, 70°, light south wind
  • What's at stake: A Lamar win clinches its first Southland title since 1971; a McNeese win gives the Cowboys their first conference winning season since 2019

Featured · Battle Row Bazaar takes over Railroad Avenue

Handmade goods, live music, and a good cause — right in the middle of North Lake Charles.

Before the game — or instead of it — Railroad Avenue is the place to be this afternoon. The Battle Row Bazaar runs 2 to 8 p.m. along Railroad Avenue from Kirkman Street to Reid Street, with local artists and makers, food trucks, live art, and a charity raffle, all hosted by the Melissa Macer Foundation.

Live music comes from Bayou Sol and DJ Secret Stache. It's the kind of afternoon that works as a whole family outing or a quick stop on the way to Navarre — and every dollar spent with a local maker stays right here.

Business Spotlight · All Put Together

Downtown Lake Charles has a shop worth a stop before the holiday rush really hits. All Put Together, a family-owned boutique at 311 Broad St., stocks curated Louisiana brands alongside apparel for men and women, trendy accessories, and gifts — the kind of place where you walk in for one thing and walk out with three.

It's a good bet for game-day gear and Christmas lists alike: Louisiana-made goods, Swig tumblers, D'Marie drink mixes, Cajun beef jerky, and gift baskets among the offerings, with free delivery around town. The owners get praised in reviews for being welcoming and quick to deliver. Open today 10 a.m.–4 p.m.

One Good Thing

Laiyana Perry is back at school — cancer-free. Students and staff at Lake Charles College Prep celebrated this week as Laiyana returned to campus after completing her cancer treatment. A whole school turning out to welcome one kid back: that's the kind of thing that reminds you what a community is for.

Road Watch

No closures are scheduled during Thanksgiving week (Nov. 22–28); nightly closures resume Nov. 30–Dec. 3 and Dec. 7–20. Detour via LA 378 at Exit 27 to I-210 East — or just take I-210.
