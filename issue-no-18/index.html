The Lead · Black Friday, but make it local

Everybody knows the Black Friday picture: a parking lot full before sunrise, a line at the door, somebody arguing over the last TV. Southwest Louisiana does that too, sure. But the better version of the day is quieter, and it's been growing here for years — a vendor market full of people who made what they're selling, a little shop on Common Street full of things somebody made by hand, a gift that nobody else in the parish is going to unwrap on Christmas morning.

That's the thing about shopping local on a day like this. The money stays here, obviously, and that's worth saying. But the gift is better, too. A pair of earrings somebody shaped by hand in Lake Charles beats a gift card every single time, and the person who made it is probably standing right there to tell you about it. So go ahead and hit the mall — the vendor market there opens at 8 — but save a stop for the small rooms, the pop-ups, and the folks who set up a table because they love making things.

And if today gets away from you, tomorrow's the day built for exactly this: Small Business Saturday, with Santa and Mrs. Claus and more than twenty vendors at Not Just Christmas. The big stores will still be there Monday. The little ones are the reason to get out of the house today. Thanksgiving week around SWLA →

Shop in the morning, laugh or rock out at night, and Christmas arrives on Saturday.

  • Ali Siddiq: The Custom Fit Tour, 7 p.m. — the comedian brings his stand-up to the Rosa Hart Theatre at the Lake Charles Event Center, 900 Lakeshore Dr. Tickets from $72.
  • Yacht Rock Night with Catalina Wine Mixer, 8 p.m. — smooth sailing into the weekend at Panorama Music House, Broad St., Lake Charles.

Free, at the Arcade Amphitheater and lakefront, 1111 Bord du Lac Dr. Bring a blanket or lawn chair — coolers and pets are not allowed.

Business Spotlight · The Magnolia Collective Vendors Market, Lake Charles

If you're shopping local today, start at the new kid on Common Street. The Magnolia Collective Vendors Market, at 4011 Common St., opened November 14 as a collection of small businesses, local artists, and vintage, antique, and thrifted finds — handmade earrings, candles, leatherwork, and a little bit of everything, all under one roof. Today's Black Friday Special runs 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., with mystery grab bags for the first 15 customers with a purchase and a drawing for a $20 gift card.
