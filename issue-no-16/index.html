The Lead · Turkeys instead of tickets

If a Calcasieu Parish deputy pulled you over Tuesday, there was a decent chance you drove off with dinner instead of a ticket. About 20 deputies spent the day handing out frozen turkeys to drivers they'd stopped for small stuff — a rolled stop sign, a busted headlight. No citation, just a bird and a happy Thanksgiving.

It's the 12th year of the program, and the turkeys — about 100 of them — came from Caran and Harry Shaheen, who own 5 Star Convenience Stores. Sheriff "Stitch" Guillory says the Shaheen family, from DeQuincy, donates the birds every year because they want to spread a little holiday cheer. "A lot of times when the police pull you over, you expect the worst," Guillory said. "And instead of giving them a ticket, they give them the turkey."

It's a small thing, really — one turkey, one driver, one less thing to worry about before Thursday. But that's kind of the point. The day before Thanksgiving is when the whole parish starts looking out for each other, and yesterday the folks in green were doing it from the side of the road. Read the KPLC story →

Featured · A little Broadway comes to the Golden Nugget on Friday

Friday night, the Golden Nugget gets a little Broadway. Leslie Odom Jr. — Tony and Grammy winner, and the original Aaron Burr in Hamilton — brings his Christmas Tour to the Grand Event Center at 8:30 p.m. It's the third year he's taken his Christmas albums on the road as a live show, a project that started back in 2016 when fans kept telling him his voice was made for Christmas songs.

He's not just flying in for the show, either. Odom told KPLC he and his band are coming in a day or two early to eat their way around Southwest Louisiana and catch the local music scene — and if a local band catches their ear, they might just get invited up on stage. That's the kind of thing that only happens when a touring act actually likes the town it's playing.

Tickets are on sale now through the Golden Nugget. If you're still looking for a Friday night plan after the turkey leftovers, this is a pretty good one. Show details and tickets →

Business Spotlight · Bayou Rum Distillery, Lacassine

If you've got somebody on your list who's impossible to shop for, take a drive east to Lacassine. Bayou Rum Distillery — the largest privately owned rum distillery in the country — sits right off I-10 in the cane fields at 20909 Frontage Road, and its free tours run Tuesday through Saturday, 9 to 5, with the last tour at 4. You watch Louisiana sugarcane become copper-pot rum, taste a few at the bar, and the gift shop has bottles and souvenirs you won't find at the grocery store. It's a local gift with a story behind it — just check the holiday hours before you head out. Plan a visit →

One Good Thing

Somebody in DeRidder is winning the holiday season, and it's a goose. The Pine Street Goose — an inflatable bird with an AirTag inside — has been getting snatched from one Pine Street business to the next, and the whole street is in on it. Brittany McVicker, who owns Dear Me Southern Boutique, started it to put a spotlight on the small businesses, and the faces behind them, up and down Pine Street. The goose has already been "booked in" at the sheriff's office, and Dylan Terry of Front Porch Pick'n says he's been up till 3 or 4 in the morning chasing it down. There's even a People's Choice award for the best snatch-and-grab video — and McVicker hopes to make it an annual tradition. Read the KPLC story →
